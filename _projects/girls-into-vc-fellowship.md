## Overview

**Girls Into VC** is a competitive fellowship designed to close the gender gap in venture capital by training the next generation of female investors. I was selected for the Berkeley chapter cohort.

## My Contribution

- Received training in **due diligence** - how to evaluate startups across technical, market, and team dimensions
- Studied **VC finance** - fund mechanics, portfolio construction, carried interest, and LP dynamics
- Conducted **investment research** and **industry analysis** on sectors of interest
- Built a global network of female leaders in innovation and entrepreneurship

## Takeaways

The fellowship gave me a structured framework for thinking about early-stage investment that I now apply to my work at Hustle Fund and my broader thinking about the biotech venture landscape.
