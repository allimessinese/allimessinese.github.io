## What I Learned

Team Epoch was where I learned that leadership is not a single skill but a set of very different ones, and that moving between them requires actively unlearning what made you effective at the previous stage.

As Co-COO I was running operations: coordinating departments, keeping projects moving, making sure the day-to-day worked. The skills that mattered were clarity, follow-through, and the ability to hold a lot of moving parts in your head at once. As Business Mentor, those same instincts became a liability. The job was no longer to do the work but to help someone else figure out how to do it, which requires a different kind of patience and a different relationship to outcomes. You succeed when they succeed, on a timeline you do not control.

The Strategy Committee role was different again. The question shifted from how do we execute this to what should we be doing and why. That kind of thinking requires a longer time horizon and a tolerance for ambiguity that operational roles actively train out of you. Relearning how to sit with open questions, without immediately converting them into tasks, was the most demanding part of the transition.

The other thing Team Epoch taught me was how much an organisation's culture depends on the people in the middle layer, not just the top. The most effective thing I did as Business Mentor was not any individual piece of advice but consistently modelling a way of approaching problems: with curiosity, rigour, and a bias toward specificity. That seemed to propagate.

## About Team Epoch

**Team Epoch** is an AI-focused student organisation at TU Delft, operating as part of the university's D:DREAM programme, which supports student-led teams working on technical, competition-driven challenges.

The team competes in data science and machine learning competitions across sectors including business, sports, autonomous transport, and environmental science, with the dual aim of producing high-quality AI solutions and training the next generation of AI engineers. The team of 27 draws primarily from Computer Science and Engineering at TU Delft but deliberately reaches across disciplines, reflecting the reality that applied AI requires more than technical expertise.

## Roles and Progression

### Partnership Manager

My entry point into Team Epoch was in partnerships, where I built and executed the team's external engagement strategy.

- Raised **€15,000** in funding and secured in-kind donations and sponsorships from industry partners
- Negotiated **5+** strategic collaborations, building the relationships that gave the team access to external resources, events, and networks

### Co-Chief Operating Officer *(Aug 2021 to Sep 2022)*

As Co-COO I took on operational responsibility for the full organisation: a team of 40 across four departments (AI, Communications, Partnerships, and Finance).

- Improved cross-department collaboration and boosted project completion rates by **20%**
- Created proposals and marketing materials contributing to a **25%** increase in external partner engagement and visibility at networking events
- Built the internal structures that allowed departments to operate with greater autonomy and coordination simultaneously

### Business Mentor *(Sep 2022 to Jul 2023)*

After stepping back from day-to-day operations, I moved into a mentoring role for the Partnership Department.

- Mentored the team in securing **5+** strategic partnerships, guiding proposal development and negotiation
- Provided business development guidance on marketing and fundraising strategy, contributing to a **20%** increase in external funding
- Coached members on market research, pitching, and networking, improving partnership success rates by **15%**

### Chairperson of the Strategy Committee *(Aug 2023 to May 2024)*

In my final role, I led the cross-functional effort to define and execute Team Epoch's year-long strategic direction.

- Developed a strategic plan aligning short and long-term goals, enhancing team performance by **25%**
- Facilitated collaboration across **4+** departments, ensuring the framework reflected the organisation's full range of needs
- Drove strategic initiatives that improved team synergy and increased project completion rates by **15%**
