## What I Learned

Being selected alongside people from every discipline (medicine, law, engineering, economics) clarified something about how I want to work. The students on this list are not specialists who stayed inside one lane. They moved between fields, built things, and took on problems that did not come with a defined method. Seeing that pattern reflected back as the selection criterion made me more deliberate about continuing to do the same.

## The Award

The **Nova 111 Student List** is published annually by Nova, a European talent network that identifies outstanding student talent across the continent. Each country list selects 111 students across categories spanning science, technology, business, the arts, and public life. Selection is based on academic achievement, entrepreneurial activity, research, and real-world impact.

I was selected as the **vertical winner** in Other Engineering and Technology for Italy, recognised for work spanning synthetic biology research, biotech GTM, science communication, and cross-disciplinary projects across multiple countries. The credential was issued in March 2025.
