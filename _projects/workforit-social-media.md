## Overview

**WorkForIt** is a platform connecting students and companies. As Social Media Manager, I owned the LinkedIn channel - building an editorial strategy from scratch and growing it into an effective distribution channel.

## My Contribution

- Developed and executed a comprehensive **LinkedIn editorial plan**, targeting both students and companies as audience segments
- Increased LinkedIn **engagement by 30%** through targeted, high-relevance content creation
- Collaborated with the founder to optimize platform presence, incorporating best practices and platform trends
- **Analyzed social media metrics** to assess campaign effectiveness, making data-driven adjustments that improved audience reach by 25%

## What I Learned

This role taught me the fundamentals of content strategy and audience development - skills I've since applied to GTM work. Effective content isn't about volume; it's about resonance with a specific audience.
