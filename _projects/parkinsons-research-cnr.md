## Overview

At the **Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche (CNR)** in Segrate, Italy — Italy's national research council — I assisted researchers investigating **Parkinson's Disease**, a neurodegenerative disorder affecting millions worldwide.

This was my first hands-on experience in a research lab, and it cemented my interest in biomedical science.

## My Contribution

- Assisted in conducting experiments and collecting data for Parkinson's Disease research
- Performed **histology** — tissue preparation and staining for microscopic analysis — and **biochemical experiments** to characterize disease-related changes
- Contributed to a **15% increase in data accuracy** through meticulous analysis and interpretation
- Contributed to research papers and presentations, communicating findings to the scientific community

## Context

This internship took place while I was still in secondary school — my earliest direct exposure to lab research. The experience of working alongside professional researchers investigating a real disease shaped my decision to study Nanobiology at TU Delft.
