## Overview

**Hustle Fund** is a pre-seed VC firm with a thesis around speed and hustle. Their **Angel Squad** is a community program that lets operators, founders, and early-career investors co-invest alongside the fund.

## My Role

- Writing angel checks into early-stage startups as part of the Angel Squad
- Developing investment thesis and due diligence skills in a community of experienced investors
- Focused on biotech, deep tech, and founder-market fit

## Context

This is part of my broader exploration of where venture capital and deep biotech intersect. I believe the best investors in biotech are those who deeply understand both the science and the commercial dynamics — which is exactly the perspective I'm building.
