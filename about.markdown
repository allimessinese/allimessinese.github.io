<div class="page-header">
  <h1>About</h1>
</div>

<p class="about-intro">
I'm Allegra — bioengineer, builder, and chronic learner. If you must know one thing about me: I'd rather be the dumbest person in the room than the smartest. I thrive when I'm surrounded by ambitious, curious, driven people — it keeps me sharp and always moving forward.
</p>
<p class="about-intro">
I graduated from UC Berkeley with an MEng in Bioengineering after a BS in Nanobiology from TU Delft. I now work in growth at <strong>Mithrl</strong>, where I get to apply everything I've learned across research, partnerships, and customer discovery to scale something that matters. I'm drawn to the intersection of deep tech and commercial impact — not just how something works, but whether and how the world actually needs it.
</p>
<p class="about-intro">
My background spans wet lab research (synthetic biology, organoids, cardiac implants), GTM, venture capital, and running operations for a 40-person student organization. I've worked in the Netherlands, Spain, and the US, and I'm building toward a career at the crossroads of biotech, startups, and venture.
</p>
