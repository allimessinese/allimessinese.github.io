On the other side: engineered biology, validated platforms, the science that can actually close those gaps. I learned that language in the lab. I use it in the room where decisions get made. The Perspective

The Patient Doesn't Move

There's a version of working in techbio where the goal quietly becomes the tool. You optimise the pipeline. You ship the feature. You celebrate the 3x productivity gain in one workflow. None of that is wrong. It's the whole point. But somewhere between the sprint planning and the model benchmarks, the metric starts to substitute for the mission.

The patient is the north star. Every hour saved in a lab, every experiment run faster, every insight surfaced sooner: it only means something if it points there.
